About MRRM

Connecting people, places, and environmental knowledge

A community-driven monitoring initiative, established in 2018.

Meenachil River & Rain Monitoring (MRRM) is a community-driven environmental monitoring initiative established in 2018 as a joint initiative of the Meenachil River Protection Council and Bhoomika.

MRRM was established with a shared commitment to understanding rainfall patterns, monitoring river conditions, and strengthening community preparedness for weather-related challenges. By bringing together environmental awareness, local knowledge, systematic observation, and scientific research, the initiative seeks to build a better understanding of the relationship between rainfall, rivers, and the communities that depend on them.

Through the active participation of local communities and volunteers, MRRM supports the collection and study of rainfall and river-level observations across different locations. These observations help build a valuable record of local environmental conditions, supporting researchers, environmental organisations, and communities in understanding changing weather patterns and their potential impacts.

Recognising the importance of accessible and reliable information, MRRM has also embraced digitalisation through a dedicated web application that helps organise, maintain, and present collected observations. The GeoFinder application further supports geographical studies by helping identify locations accurately and obtain latitude and longitude coordinates, improving the geographical understanding of environmental observations.

At its heart, MRRM is about more than monitoring rainfall. It is about connecting people, knowledge, and environmental information to encourage informed decisions, strengthen preparedness, and contribute to the long-term protection of our rivers and natural resources.

Our Vision

A well-informed, resilient community

To build a well-informed, environmentally conscious and resilient community where reliable rainfall and river observations contribute to the protection of water resources, improved preparedness for natural hazards, and a sustainable future for generations to come.

Our Mission

What MRRM works towards

Monitor and understand

Encourage systematic observation of rainfall and river levels to better understand local weather patterns and changing environmental conditions.

Empower communities

Encourage community participation and volunteer engagement, fostering shared responsibility for rivers and natural resources.

Support research and awareness

Make organised observations useful to researchers, environmental organisations, and communities studying rainfall, river behaviour, and related risks.

Embrace digital innovation

Use accessible digital tools to organise, preserve, and share environmental observations.

Strengthen geographical understanding

Use GeoFinder to support accurate location identification and relate environmental observations to specific places.

Promote preparedness and conservation

Contribute to awareness of weather-related risks, informed decisions, and responsible protection of rivers and surrounding environments.

Our Approach

Four parts of community monitoring

MRRM connects local participation with systematic observation, useful digital tools, and care for the environment.

Community participation

We value local volunteers and communities observing environmental conditions and building a shared understanding of rainfall and river behaviour.

Observation and research

We encourage systematic data collection and the study of local weather and river conditions to support environmental understanding and research.

Digital transformation

Our web application helps organise and present observations, while GeoFinder supports location identification and geographical studies.

Environmental responsibility

We encourage informed community action, environmental awareness, and the long-term protection of rivers and water resources.

Our Commitment

Understanding our environment is a step towards protecting it

By combining community participation, environmental observation, research, and digital innovation, MRRM strives to contribute meaningfully to the study of rainfall and river conditions. As a joint initiative of the Meenachil River Protection Council and Bhoomika, we remain committed to environmental awareness, knowledge-sharing, and helping communities build a safer, more sustainable relationship with their natural surroundings.
