The initiative

Rooted in community, grown through collaboration

Native Window is a Bhoomika Charitable Society initiative that connects local farmers, small-scale entrepreneurs, consumers, and visitors through a shared platform for local produce and traditional food.

Local produce, shared opportunity

A window into Poonjar’s food and farming

With a network of more than 50 farmers, Native Window provides a common marketing platform for locally grown fruits, vegetables, and tubers. It also brings homemade products—such as pickles, traditional snacks, and chips—to local customers and visitors.

Managed by a local youth, the initiative helps farmers market their produce, supports women engaged in food processing and value addition, and encourages small-scale rural entrepreneurship. Reasonably priced local food creates employment for rural youth and introduces tourists to the region’s native agricultural produce and traditional food culture. Native Window also serves as a local tourism information centre, strengthening the connection between agriculture, community development, and rural tourism.
