Native Collectives

Women and farmers working together to build skills, small enterprises, and stronger livelihoods.

The initiative

Rooted in community, grown through collaboration

Bhoomika’s Native Collectives bring local women and farmers together around shared production, skills, and livelihood goals. Each collective is rooted in its community and built on local resources, traditional knowledge, and member participation.

Member-led

Each group is rooted in its community and shaped by its members.

Shared learning

Members work together and learn practical skills from one another.

Local resources

Collectives build on local produce, resources, and traditional knowledge.

Rural livelihoods

Small enterprises create income and opportunity close to home.

Many groups, shared purpose

Local skills become local livelihoods

The collectives create a space for people to work together, learn from one another, and build small enterprises close to home. Members draw on local produce, practical skills, and traditional knowledge to create products, income, and dignity.

Across these groups, Bhoomika works with more than 100 native collective members. Their work is community-led, grounded in participation, and shaped by the goals and resources of each collective.
