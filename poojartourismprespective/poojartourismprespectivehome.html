Poonjar Tourism Perspective

Poonjar, in Kottayam district, Kerala, is rich in natural beauty, cultural heritage, agricultural traditions, and rural life. Through this initiative, Bhoomika Charitable Society promotes responsible, community-based, and courteous tourism that connects visitors with local people, nature, and traditional ways of life.

The initiative showcases Poonjar’s identity through its agricultural practices, local products, traditional food, scenic landscapes, and cultural experiences. Respectful exchanges between visitors and residents help create meaningful travel while supporting local farmers, women’s collectives, and small-scale entrepreneurs.

By encouraging environmental awareness and preserving local traditions, Bhoomika aims to strengthen community livelihoods. Tourism can be a pathway to community development, cultural preservation, and environmental responsibility—so every visitor can experience the warmth, hospitality, and authentic spirit of Poonjar.
